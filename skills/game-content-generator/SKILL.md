# Game Content Generator Skill

This Agent Skill guides the generation of creative, balanced, and engine-ready specifications for browser mini-games, falling-item arcade games, matching games, and collection challenges.

## Capabilities

1. **Game Concept Generation**: Create engaging arcade game themes, titles, visual palettes, and audio cues.
2. **Quest & Challenge Systems**: Formulate level objectives, score targets, combo milestones, and star rating criteria.
3. **Item & Hazard Balance**: Design positive items, negative hazards, and special powerups with tuned spawn rates and point values.
4. **Pacing & Difficulty Scaling**: Construct time-based difficulty curves for speed multipliers, wave rushes, and item density.

---

## 1. Skill Workflow & Procedure

When invoked to generate content for a game engine or design new mini-games:

1. **Define Core Concept**: Select a distinct theme (e.g. Space Odyssey, Candy Sweets, Deep Sea Diving, Cyberpunk).
2. **Set Objectives & Star Ratings**:
   - 1-Star Threshold: Minimum target score (e.g. 50 pts).
   - 2-Star Threshold: Mastery score (e.g. 80 pts).
   - 3-Star Threshold: Perfect/Jackpot score (e.g. 120 pts).
3. **Item Catalog Creation**:
   - Generate at least 3-5 positive items (`good`) ranging from common (+1 pt) to rare (+15 pts).
   - Generate at least 3-4 negative hazards (`bad`) ranging from minor (-1 pt) to severe (-10 pts / life loss).
   - Include special powerups (`magnet`, `chrono`, `shield`, `mystery`).
4. **Pacing Curve**:
   - Stage 1 (0-15s): Orientation speed (1.0x).
   - Stage 2 (15-30s): Acceleration wave (1.25x).
   - Stage 3 (30-45s): Speed surge rush (1.55x).
   - Stage 4 (45-60s): Climax surge (1.9x).
